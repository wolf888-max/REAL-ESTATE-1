FAQs

Common Questions

Do you charge commission to buyers?

Our commission structure follows standard Lahore real estate practice and is discussed upfront before any visit — there are never hidden charges added at the end of a deal.

Can you help outside Sabzazar?

Yes — while we're based in Sabzazar, our listings and network cover DHA, Bahria Town, Johar Town, Wapda Town, Lake City and more across Lahore.

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

Most calls and WhatsApp messages are answered within the hour during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM–8 PM) — our reviews consistently mention this.

Do you verify property titles before listing?

Every property is physically inspected and checked for clear title before it's added to our listings — we won't show you something we haven't verified ourselves.

Do you help with rentals as well as sales?

Yes — we handle rentals for both tenants and owners, including tenant screening, lease drafting and move-in inspections.
