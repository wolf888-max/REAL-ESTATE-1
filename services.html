For Buyers

Buying a Property

Whether it's a first 5 Marla home or a 2 Kanal investment, we shortlist options that actually match your budget and purpose — not just whatever is easiest to sell. Every property is checked before we ever schedule a visit.

  • Personalized shortlist based on budget & area
  • Accompanied site visits
  • Price negotiation on your behalf
  • Title verification before token money
For Sellers

Selling Your Property

We price your property against real, recent transactions in your block — not wishful thinking — then put it in front of a network of active, serious buyers so it doesn't sit for months.

  • Free, honest market valuation
  • Professional photos & listing write-up
  • Access to our verified buyer network
  • Full support through transfer & registry
For Owners & Tenants

Renting Made Easy

Owners get tenant screening and lease management without the daily hassle. Renters get listings that are actually available and priced fairly — no bait-and-switch.

  • Tenant background & reference checks
  • Lease agreement drafting
  • Rent collection coordination
  • Move-in & move-out inspections
