Our Story

Built in Sabzazar, Trusted Across Lahore

A property dealership run on straightforward advice, quick answers, and doing right by every client — whether it's a first 5 Marla home or a 2 Kanal investment.

Who We Are

Real Estate, the Way It Should Work

AL-Ghous Real Estate operates out of Al Nafey Center on Sabzazar's main commercial zone — a location we chose because it puts us where our clients already are. Over the years we've helped families find their first home, guided investors toward plots before prices moved, and managed rentals for owners who just want it handled properly.

What keeps clients coming back isn't a big marketing budget — it's a 5.0 rating built one honest deal at a time. No inflated listings, no disappearing after the token money changes hands.

  • Physically verified listings only
  • Same-day response on calls & WhatsApp
  • Support through registry & legal transfer
